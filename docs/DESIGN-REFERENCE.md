# FINN visual reference

Reference: [HopeRise — Charity Landing Page Design, Phenomenon Labs / Phenomenon Studio](https://dribbble.com/shots/26290772-HopeRise-Charity-Landing-Page-Design).

The supplied reference was inspected visually, including the static hero and animated layout. FINN follows its warm cream/sand palette, oversized black grotesk typography, inline photographic capsule, minimal horizontal navigation, green pill actions, thin separators and large editorial figures. The former dark sidebar and layered dashboard styling were replaced.

The content and interactions remain investor-specific: portfolio briefing, dated financial evidence, company lookup, on-demand research, management-guidance investigation, market observations and source details. The charity's brand, patient images and donation content are not used.

Typography: Inter Tight for display headings, Inter for body and controls. The capsule photograph depicts Mumbai and is credited to Iqbal Alam on Unsplash in the footer.

Responsive checks: desktop layout and 390px phone width; no horizontal overflow observed. Verified controls: briefing scroll action, briefing-to-event navigation, company research navigation and blank-question investigation. Financial processing and AI Studio configuration remain unchanged by this visual update.


3 Nov 2026 revision: replaced the reference-led photography hero with a conventional investor workspace at the user's request. Palette now uses muted sage, off-white and dark forest green; headings and body use Inter. No external photograph is used, so the visible photography credit was removed. Research prioritizes the answer, then identifiable primary-document cards and optional supporting excerpts. Technical coverage details are collapsed rather than presented as red error blocks.
