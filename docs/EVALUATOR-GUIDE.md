# Try FINN in five minutes

Open https://finn-investment-research-86hh.onrender.com/ on a phone or desktop. No sign-in is required. A free hosting cold start can take roughly a minute.

1. **Discover → investigate.** Start with the clearly labeled historical Infosys briefing. Open the results event, then investigate the guidance. The timeline preserves original guidance, revisions and full-year outcome with company-published sources.
2. **Research → follow up.** Open Company research. Choose Infosys and ask "What are the key cash flow and business risks for Infosys in FY26?" Inspect the cited sources, then use **Ask a follow-up** to challenge a risk or financial point. Prior company/thread context is retained.
3. **Reuse → resume.** Open History, reopen the report and revisit an earlier turn. Repeat the same question: a successful saved answer is reused for 24 hours. Refresh explicitly checks newer disclosures.
4. **Try another company.** Enter DIXON using Find company (it is outside the default watchlist), then ask "Assess FY26 revenue, profitability and investment risks." For MARUTI, ask for a business-performance and risk overview. These are demonstrations of one general discovery pipeline, not separate company implementations.
5. **Inspect current observations.** Check current filings in the briefing, or use Market check in research. Price/volume thresholds are configurable. These checks happen on request; they are not a continuous alert service.

The latest live check produced 11 claims across 4 source documents for Dixon and a fresh 9-claim Maruti report across 2 sources. Results and latency vary with source/provider availability.

## When a provider is unavailable

The app shows an availability explanation and original sources, retains a previous successful report and offers retry. Cached reports and the reviewed guidance example remain useful. A source outage is not represented as no news or no risk.
